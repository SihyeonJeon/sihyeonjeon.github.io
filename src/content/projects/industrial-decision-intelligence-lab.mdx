## Problem

Forecast error is not the decision. Inventory policy needs the cost and service trade-off after the forecast is used

## Built

- UCI Online Retail II transaction pipeline
- SKU daily demand aggregation
- seasonal baseline and model forecast
- base-stock policy under lead time
- inventory simulation with cost, stockout, and service metrics
- cost-service frontier across service quantiles
