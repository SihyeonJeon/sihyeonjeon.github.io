## Why this exists

Runtime choices are often argued with vague speed claims. This lab will publish methodology first, then numbers: p50 and p95 latency, memory footprint, and quality deltas.

## Planned proof

- reproducible hardware and model configuration
- tokens/sec and memory tables
- quantization comparison with quality notes
- variance report across repeated runs

## Acceptance bar

No performance claim is promoted until the measurement script, environment, and raw result table are public.
