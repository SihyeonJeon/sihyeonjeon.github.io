## Why this exists

Prediction is not the final product in many industrial settings. The useful artifact is a decision under constraints, with enough traceability to explain how a scenario changed the output.

## Planned proof

- synthetic but realistic planning dataset
- forecast-to-optimization pipeline
- scenario comparison dashboard
- decision trace for every recommendation

## Acceptance bar

The project becomes measured only when a user can rerun the scenario and reproduce the recommended decision.
