<h2 data-i18n-en="Problem" data-i18n-ko="문제">Problem</h2>

<p data-i18n-en="Forecast-to-policy test on retail demand data; a better forecast still produced service risk on one SKU; forecast error was not enough, the decision needed cost and service after simulation" data-i18n-ko="retail demand data로 forecast-to-policy 테스트; 더 좋은 예측도 한 SKU에서는 service risk를 만들었음; forecast error만으로는 부족했고 simulation 이후 cost와 service를 함께 확인">Forecast-to-policy test on retail demand data; a better forecast still produced service risk on one SKU; forecast error was not enough, the decision needed cost and service after simulation</p>

<h2 data-i18n-en="Built" data-i18n-ko="구성">Built</h2>

- UCI Online Retail II transaction pipeline
- SKU daily demand aggregation
- seasonal baseline and model forecast
- base-stock policy under lead time
- inventory simulation with cost, stockout, and service metrics
- cost-service frontier across service quantiles
- 36-scenario sensitivity grid
- SKU-level service-risk diagnostics
